## 1. Positioning & Messaging

### Positioning statement
**SAGE Makine is the specialist, in-region supplier of X-ray food inspection and color sorting (renk ayırma) machines for Turkey's food processors — run by a founder with 22 years inside the industry, based in Manisa, the heart of Turkey's dried-fruit export basin, with local installation, training, technical service and spare-parts stock.**

Strategic frame: both direct competitors are weak generalists (EBD = unfinished inspection-equipment catalog with zero food-sector targeting and **no color sorter at all**; DenetGöz = fragmented multi-brand integrator whose specs live on a bot-blocked second domain). SAGE wins by being the **deep, focused, local specialist**: only two product families, six named customer sectors, real specs published openly, real service promises. The closest business-model rival to monitor is **MES Turkey** (Techik TR office — also sells X-ray + color sorter).

### Value propositions (5)
1. **22 yıl sektör tecrübesi** — the founder knows the customer's *product*, not just the machine (çöp-sap in sultanas, aflatoxin in figs, shell fragments in hazelnut kernels, same-size stones in lentils). "Sektörün içinden gelen tedarikçi."
2. **Manisa'dan aynı gün servis** — located inside the Aegean dried-fruit basin (92% of sultana production, ~62% of dried-fruit exports flow through Ege). Seasonal processors (Aug–Jan peak) cannot tolerate downtime; SAGE is hours away, not a continent away.
3. **İki teknoloji, tek çözüm ortağı** — color sorter + X-ray are *complementary* (optical catches color/shape/light shell defects; X-ray catches density contaminants: stone, glass, metal, pit fragments, bone). SAGE designs the combined line.
4. **İhracat güvencesi** — machines positioned as insurance against EU border rejections (Turkey is #1 in RASFF notifications; one rejected container costs more than a sorting machine) and as the equipment BRCGS/IFS/HACCP audits effectively require.
5. **Şeffaflık + somut taahhütler** — full spec tables published openly (competitors hide them), sample-test offer ("ürününüzü gönderin, test edelim"), explicit warranty/service/spare-parts commitments, NDK radiation-licensing paperwork handled by SAGE.

- H1: **"Ürününüzdeki Son Yabancı Maddeye Kadar."**

**Option C (local authority):**

### Trust signals to display site-wide
- **"22 Yıllık Sektör Tecrübesi"** (oversized numeral, stats strip) — beats EBD's vague "10 yılı aşkın"
- **"Manisa'dan Tüm Türkiye'ye"** + service map (Ege same-day; Bursa/Afyon, Mersin/Gaziantep, Karadeniz coverage rings)
- **Yerinde servis + yedek parça stoğu + kurulum ve operatör eğitimi dahil** — stated explicitly with response-time commitment (e.g. "Ege'de 24 saat, Türkiye genelinde 48 saat içinde yerinde müdahale" — confirm with founder before publishing)
- **Garanti** (state the term explicitly; DenetGöz's "2 yıl, 5'e uzatılabilir" is the benchmark to match)
- **CE belgeleri + NDK lisans desteği** ("X-Ray cihazınızın Nükleer Düzenleme Kurumu lisans sürecini biz yönetiyoruz")
- **Numune testi:** "Ürününüzü gönderin, kendi makinemizde ayıklayalım, sonucu birlikte görelim."
- Header: phone + **WhatsApp click-to-chat** always visible (Turkish B2B buyers call); "7/24 Destek Hattı" if the founder can honor it
- Real founder name + photo on Hakkımızda; real references with logos/quotes as soon as first installs exist — **never placeholder content** (EBD's fatal flaw)

## 6. Design direction notes

### Patterns to adopt (from TOMRA, Key Technology, Mekitec, Sesotec)
- **Mekitec-scale IA**: product-first, focused, Service & Support as its own top-level nav item.
- **Homepage anatomy**: hero (video/action photo + bold claim + 2 CTAs) → **stats strip immediately after hero** (oversized numerals) → sector tile grid with food photography (the visual centerpiece) → product-family cards → service band with technician photo → testimonials → **contact form embedded on the page**, not just linked.
- **Product page template**: hero w/ "Teklif Alın" + "Broşür İndir" → (customer quote when available) → demo video → 4 benefit cards → named tech features → "Neleri tespit eder/ayıklar" icon list → **open, complete spec table (Mekitec-style — unlike TOMRA/Key who gate specs; openness is our trust play)** → application tiles → ungated PDF → embedded form.
- **Visual language**: blue + white + gray industrial base; color arrives via **warm food photography** (green pistachios, amber sultanas, golden fries) against neutral machine chrome. One geometric sans-serif (Inter or Manrope), big bold headlines, generous whitespace, oversized stat numerals, alternating full-width image/text bands, icon rows. Restraint = engineering credibility.
- **Sorting-in-action video is the single most convincing asset in this industry** — embed short clips per application (DenetGöz's YouTube demos are their best asset and their site barely uses them; do it properly).
- Borrow from competitors: sticky **WhatsApp CTA on every product page** (EBD's one good instinct), header phone + "7/24 destek" positioning, quantified hero claim pattern (DenetGöz's "Minimum Fire, Maksimum Kazanç") — but substantiated.
- **Avoid**: slogan carousels (Satake), conglomerate mega-menus (Bühler), emoji-as-icons and template artifacts (DenetGöz), any placeholder/test content (EBD's credibility killer), exposed view counters, half-built English version.
